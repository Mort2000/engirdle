The Quiet Practice of Mending
Elena Marsh · 约 4,200 词 · 句级语音 9:03
The workshop smelled of cedar and old glue. On the bench lay a chair that had survived three generations, its joints loose but its story intact. Martin ran his thumb along the worn armrest and smiled. “People throw away things that only need an hour of patience,” he said.
He believes that mending is a form of attention. When you repair a chair, you learn how it was made, and you honor the hands that made it. The work is slow, but slowness is the point.
